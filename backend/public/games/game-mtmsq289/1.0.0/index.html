How to play

  • Use the joystick at the bottom to aim and shoot in any direction.
  • Push upward to throw away toward the hoop; use less deflection for near throws.

  • Release the joystick to fire the basketball.
  • Nothing but net scores a SWISH, and back-to-back baskets build a streak.
  • You have 45 seconds on the shot clock.
