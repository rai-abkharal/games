SPY SHOOTER - MR. BULLET (OFFLINE EDITION)
===========================================

How to Play:
1. Double click "index.html" in any web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari, Opera).
2. Works 100% offline without internet connection or local server.
3. Supported on PC, Mac, Laptop, Android, and iOS devices.

Controls:
- Touch & Drag / Click & Hold: Aim laser sight and set trajectory.
- Release: Fire weapon (ricochet bullets off walls, blocks, and TNT).
- Top Left Button: Sound Mute / Unmute.
- Top Right Button: Retry / Restart current level.

Enjoy the 40 puzzle levels!
